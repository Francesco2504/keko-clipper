Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Keko Clipper ("the tool") is an internal tool used only by the people who run the YouTube channel @KekoClips25. It uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the tool

Only the operators of the @KekoClips25 channel. There is no public sign-up and no other users.

What data we access

  • Google OAuth token for the @KekoClips25 channel, with the scope youtube.upload, used only to upload our own approved videos.
  • Video metadata we write ourselves (title, description, tags) and the ID of each uploaded video, to keep track of what was published.

We do not access, collect or store any data of other YouTube users, viewers or channels. We do not read comments, analytics, subscribers or private account information through the API.

How we use, store and share it

  • The OAuth token is stored encrypted in our own self-hosted automation server (n8n), which is not publicly accessible.
  • Upload records (clip name, title, YouTube video ID, date) are stored in our private database.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any of this data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies and device information

The tool does not place cookies or collect device information from anyone. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Upload records are kept while the channel is active and deleted on request. Access can be revoked at any time from Google security settings; after revocation the token stops working and is deleted from our server within 7 days. Stored data obtained through the API is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days if it is no longer needed.

Contact

For any question about this policy, open an issue at github.com/Francesco2504/keko-clipper.
